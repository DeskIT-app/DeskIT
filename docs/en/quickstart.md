# Quick start — ten steps

The whole guide for the reader who never scrolls. Every step names the
screen exactly as DeskIT draws it.

1. **Get it.** With winget: open a terminal and run `winget install YoavShimron.DeskIT`.
   Without it: download `DeskIT-Setup-x.y.z.exe` from the
   [latest release](https://github.com/DeskIT-app/DeskIT/releases/latest). If Windows says
   "Windows protected your PC", press **More info**, then **Run anyway** — [chapter 1](01-install)
   says why.
2. **Install.** No administrator password is asked. Pick Hebrew or English for the installer
   and press through. DeskIT starts by itself at the end.
3. **Welcome.** Read the three sentences. Press **Next**.
4. **Microphone.** Speak; the bar should move. If it does not, press **Open the Windows setting**,
   allow desktop apps to use the microphone, come back. **Next**.
5. **This computer.** The top line says what DeskIT found in your PC. Press **Download**
   (1.62 GB, from huggingface.co, into your DeskIT folder; you can press **Next** without it and
   download from Home another day). If the page offers **Use the NVIDIA card**, leave it on for
   fast transcription (1.37 GB more).
6. **Say one sentence.** Press **Record 3 seconds**, say one Hebrew sentence, read what it
   heard and how long it took. On a PC without an NVIDIA card this is the speed to expect.
7. **Keys.** These are the keyboard keys DeskIT listens to. Keep them unless one clashes with a
   program you use — the **Keys** place changes them later. **Next**.
8. **Optional extras.** Five switches. Nothing goes to the cloud unless you turn the first one
   on; [chapter 5](05-cloud) explains it. **Next**, then **Finish** (or **Open the desk**).
9. **Dictate.** Click into any text field, hold **Right Ctrl**, speak, release. The Hebrew text
   is pasted where the cursor is. Tap **Ctrl+F9** on a misheard word to teach DeskIT the right
   one.
10. **Check it.** Open the dashboard's **Network** place: during plain dictation the table is
    empty (or shows only `127.0.0.1`). Nothing left your PC. [Chapter 4](04-privacy) shows
    three more checks you can do in ten minutes.
