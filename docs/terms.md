# DeskIT account terms

**DRAFT — English canonical text**; the Hebrew column is the owner's
(plan chapter 16). The app itself needs no terms: it is free software
under the Apache License 2.0, which already disclaims warranty and
liability, and nothing is shown at install. This page is for the one
thing the owner operates for you — the optional account — and is shown
once, inside the account sign-up card, with a single "I agree"
checkbox. Never shown to a user who stays local.

## What the service is

Problem reports and, later, settings sync, run by Yoav Shimron on
Supabase. It is free. It may be paused or ended with notice in the
release notes; the app keeps working locally either way.

## Acceptable use

Do not abuse the report channel: no spam, no other people's data, no
content that is illegal in Israel. The owner may delete reports and
close accounts that break this.

## Your data

The privacy policy (`privacy.md`) is the binding description of what is
collected, by whom and for how long. Account deletion is immediate and
self-service from inside the app. The owner does not sell or share your
data beyond the recipients the policy names.

## Cloud features

Bring your own key: your contract for Groq or Google is with that
provider, and DeskIT is not a party to it. Those features are for
users aged 18 or over.

## No warranty, limitation

The service is provided as is, without warranty of any kind, as the
Apache License 2.0 says of the software; for a free service the
owner's liability is limited to what Israeli law allows.

## Law and venue

Israeli law; the courts of `<the owner's district>`, Israel.

## Changes

A new `text_version` asks for the checkbox again at the next use of
the account. Without agreement the account cannot be used; the app
itself keeps running.
