DeskIT-app/ivrit-turbo-real-length-ct2 — the Hebrew model on a PC without
an NVIDIA card (a modified version of ivrit.ai's whisper-large-v3-turbo)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Based on: ivrit-ai/whisper-large-v3-turbo, Copyright (c) ivrit.ai,
Apache License 2.0 (the full text is the LICENSE file beside this one);
itself a fine-tune of OpenAI's Whisper large-v3-turbo (MIT, above).
Model card: https://huggingface.co/ivrit-ai/whisper-large-v3-turbo
Changes (Apache-2.0 section 4(b)): modified by DeskIT (Yoav Shimron),
October 2026 — trained further so that it can be encoded at a
recording's own length instead of Whisper's 30-second window
(audio-context fine-tuning, the method of FUTO's whisper-acft), one
epoch with a LoRA adapter merged back into the weights, then converted
for CTranslate2 (float16). The weights in models-1 are this modified
version, not ivrit.ai's.
Training data: ivrit.ai's crowd-transcribe-v5
(https://huggingface.co/datasets/ivrit-ai/crowd-transcribe-v5) and
knesset-committees (https://huggingface.co/datasets/ivrit-ai/knesset-committees),
by ivrit.ai, under the ivrit.ai licence (v2, 2024-10-01):
Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) with ivrit.ai's terms
that the data may be used only to train AI models or for academic
research, and never for deep-fakes (https://www.ivrit.ai/en/the-license/).
The datasets themselves are not distributed; the clips used were
filtered and cut by DeskIT (knesset-committees: segments whose protocol
text misses or adds at most one spoken word, joined into clips of up to
28 s).
Not shipped: downloaded at first run from DeskIT's own release
(https://github.com/DeskIT-app/DeskIT/releases/tag/models-1) at the
revision pinned in models.lock (night15-2026-10-26), and verified by
SHA-256.
Not shipped: an optional download, pinned in models.lock.
